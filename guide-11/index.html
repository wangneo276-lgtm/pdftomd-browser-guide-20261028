What the PDF to MD Converter Is

The PDF to MD Converter is a free in-browser PDF to Markdown converter. On the page, the homepage title is "PDF to MD Converter — Free Online Tool | pdftomd.tools", displaying the eyebrow "Free in-browser converter" and the homepage H1 "PDF to MD Converter". Its byline reads: Updated Oct 20, 2026 · pdftomd.tools team. The lead states: Pdf to md turns the text inside a PDF into editable Markdown. The free tool runs in the browser with Mozilla's pdf.js, keeps headings, lists, and tables, and uploads nothing, so the result can be pasted into notes, docs, or a model chat. It is not a desktop app, not an OCR product, and not a general AI writer.

Converting pdf to md means transforming a Portable Document Format file, standardized as ISO 32000, into Markdown, the plain-text format published in 2004 and now used in READMEs, wikis, and writing apps. People use it when they need structure they can edit, version, or feed to models rather than a locked print layout. The page notes the tradeoff: editable text is gained; pixel-perfect pagination is not the goal.

The Four Conversion Steps

The tool line says: Runs in your browser · nothing uploaded. Mozilla's pdf.js processes files locally across four steps, all inside the browser tab:

  1. Drop the file — a text-based document, and the run stays on the device.
  2. Read and assemble.
  3. Preview and copy — check the preview, tidy a line if needed, then copy or save.
  4. Flip direction — open the reverse converter instead of installing another desktop app.

Retained Structure and Limits

The free path keeps headings, paragraphs, lists, and simple tables when the source has a real text layer. The checklist on the page highlights headings, paragraphs, lists, and simple tables, along with code blocks from monospace text. The pass preserves title hierarchy when the source sets headings in larger type, bullet and numbered lists, simple tables, and monospace runs as code blocks.

Certain elements are omitted. Bold and italic are not carried over yet. Figures are not exported in this free pass; a figure-heavy job needs a separate image step beside the text export. Image-only scans are not OCR'd, so they come out empty.

The homepage attributes specific results to its own tests. The homepage states that in its own test, an 11-page report exported to PDF and converted back kept all 12 second-level headings, 4 tables, and 48 paragraphs. When source documents contain real text tables, it writes a GFM pipe table. The homepage says all 12 table fixtures passed in its tests. Complex layouts may need a quick cleanup afterward. The PDF to MD Converter is designed for documents with a real text layer.
